Rejects: non-`media`/non-reactive clip types (:313), non-`{visual,audio}` track kinds (:276), ≠1 visual track (:282), visual gaps/overlaps (:348-356), overlapping audio (:362-365), audio ending past visual frame (:366-369), speed≠1 (:169), transforms/crops/effects/transitions/opacity/`muted`/fades (:163-226), missing assets/files (:678-684), visual-source embedded audio with gain≠0 (:707-715), source bounds > probed duration (:725-729). **NOT validated:** clip-level `volume`/gain fades absent `params`; audio **gaps** (silence synthesized via `anullsrc`, command.py:290-296); audio track `scale`/`fit` semantics (visual-only check, :139-144); no probe of *audio* stream fields (codec/rate/layout) for support — only presence (:703); `speed` on audio clips is rejected globally (:168-174), but reactive path re-checks (:233).

## Verified facts
Gap-filling silence; strict fail-closed media-only backend; finalizer (`finalizers/ffmpeg/run.py`) exists with `_AUDIO_TRANSCODE_FIELDS` (:69-76); command construction pure/testable.

## Unknowns
Audio-stream codec/layout validation; whether audio gaps are intentional semantics; finalizer's audio-presence rules (truncated).

## Risks
Ownership modes (PASSTHROUGH) currently only valid without audio clips — extraction must decide gap-fill vs passthrough; silent `anullsrc` audio could mask dropped streams.

## Suggested approach
Extract `support.py`+`command.py` as the backend core; add probe-driven audio-field checks; model ownership as "silence-fill | passthrough | none"; port `test_ffmpeg_backend/support` first.
