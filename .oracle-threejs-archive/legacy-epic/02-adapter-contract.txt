**1. **2. supports_windows = host-slicing.** `_segment_request` (service.py:1048-1094): if `capabilities.supports_windows is not False`, the canonical `window` passes through (1067-1068); otherwise the service materializes a per-window timeline via `_window_timeline`/`_window_clip` (1097-1172: clips rebased to `at=visible_start-start`, media `from/to` source-trimmed, ids suffixed `_start_end`, tracks filtered, `metadata.source_window_start/end_seconds` added), writes `segment-inputs/{index:04d}-timeline.json`, and sends `window: null` (1091-1092). Static support selects `supports_windows` vs `supports_full_timeline` by request.window presence (858-867).
