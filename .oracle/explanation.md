## The Astrid packification effort, explained

Astrid packification is an architectural narrowing effort. Its aspiration is to make `astrid/core/` a small, closed kernel containing only machinery the product cannot sensibly plug in from elsewhere: the CLI gateway, project/session foundations, common execution state, pack discovery and validation, capability registries, SDK surfaces, and provider-neutral protocols. Concrete tools, adapters, workflows, and optional service domains should live outside that kernel as packs. Besides making Astrid easier to understand, this creates portability seams for a future shared agent framework: manifests, multi-root discovery, validation, wheel proofs, and import rails can travel even if Astrid-specific branding and product code do not (`.oracle/plan.md:98-131`; `.oracle/inputs/openrouter-sensecheck.md:414-436`).

A capability pack today is a directory under `astrid/packs/` with a `pack.yaml` describing its identity and policy—fields such as `schema_version`, `id`, `version`, `origin`, `install_tier`, `pack_type`, domain, stability, permissions, capabilities, content, extensions, and agent metadata. Its implementation is organized into declared roots such as `executors/`, `orchestrators/`, and `elements/`, with agent guidance in `skill/SKILL.md`. Capabilities are addressed by qualified IDs such as `video_editing.cut` or `rendering.render`: `<pack>.<name>` is the product contract, and `python3 -m astrid` is the single executable gateway (`.oracle/plan.md:125-131,191-209`).

The audit found that Astrid was only partly organized that way. Roughly 80–90% of `astrid/core/` was legitimate kernel machinery, but its boundary had not been explicitly locked. A second band was unmistakably domain code living in core: Fal, Codex, and VibeComfy generation backends; experiments; RunPod maintenance; and Reigh and worker implementations (`.oracle/plan.md:213-287`). Other content bypassed packs entirely: `_core` was a manifest-less skill shell; skills used a flat directory walk and duplicate discovery path; and elements could be loaded from active themes or a legacy workspace rather than pack manifests (`.oracle/findings/01-discovery-load-graph.txt:3-7`; `.oracle/plan.md:133-189`).

There were also non-pack runnable paths. Top-level `serve`, `worker`, `runpod`, publishing, `author`, and `run` routes competed with qualified executor or orchestrator invocation. Implicit `--brief/--video` dispatch and directly runnable pack modules created still more entrances (`.oracle/plan.md:271-286,308-355`). Inside pack roots, loose Blender scripts, undeclared support directories, golden material, and fixtures exposed gaps between the documented layout and what validation actually inspected (`.oracle/findings/07-pack-hygiene.txt:7`). Outside `astrid/`, `remotion/`, themes, examples, scripts, and tests remain product-owned rather than becoming framework kernel; the tracked `fal-voice-upscale/` experiment was accidental repository debris to remove (`.oracle/plan.md:125-129,374-383`; `.oracle/findings/16-root-artifacts.txt:3-9`).

The end state is deliberately strict: one ordered metadata graph across source, local, extra, environment, installed, and wheel-source layers; manifest-backed origins for every discoverable capability, skill, and element; no direct core-to-pack imports; deterministic bundled inventory; one canonical qualified ID per capability; and no compatibility scaffolding. `_core` becomes a trusted manifest-backed system pack. `astrid/packs/bundled.yaml` defines what the product ships, while checkout-local packs remain discoverable without contaminating generated committed indexes. Structure and import checks finish with zero exemptions (`.oracle/plan.md:133-177,401-420`).

The work is divided into two approximately two-week sprints. Sprint 1 establishes the foundation: lock the kernel and lifecycle, legalize `_core`, create the bundled inventory and delete `builtin`, unify skill and element readers, prove the complete graph from an installed wheel, and remove aliases. Task 1.3 is the hard Phase 1 gate: no extraction begins until source and wheel inventory agree and every discovery layer passes (`.oracle/plan.md:3-46`; `.oracle/tasklist.md:163-198`). Sprint 2 performs the heavier extraction and closure: generation, RunPod, experiments and dependencies, Reigh and worker code, supported pack APIs, compatibility deletion, private-entrypoint cleanup, layout enforcement, documentation, hygiene, and final verification (`.oracle/plan.md:48-92`).

The shim sweep reflects the user’s instruction that Astrid should not be cautious about eliminating shims. The status summarizes 25 candidates as 20 CUT and 5 KEEP (`.oracle/status.md:15-17`). The cuts include making Arnold the sole lifecycle engine, deleting `builtin` and the production `builtin.agent_probe`, removing the `aliases:` field and resolver, deleting duplicate `serve`/`worker`/`runpod` routes, removing the `in_process` exception, dropping legacy manifest and auto-bind parsers, removing rendering’s compatibility selector, and directly renaming `rendering.legacy_hybrid` to `rendering.hybrid` (`.oracle/plan.md:422-456`). What remains has an end-state purpose rather than a migration purpose: `astrid scratch`, centralized `_core → astrid` branding, `deprecated` as metadata on one canonical ID, runtime support negotiation, and explicit forks and overrides.

These cuts preserve functionality by removing routes, names, and duplicate machinery rather than useful operations. Canonical replacements are qualified executor calls such as `runpod.ensure_storage`, `reigh.worker`, and `reigh.serve_local_bridge`. Two interpretations are intentionally narrow: `builtin.agent_probe` becomes a temporary test-pack fixture, and task-only verbs are deleted only where no Arnold-backed meaning exists (`.oracle/status.md:66-72`).

So far, all of this is planning. Megado ran a swarm audit, produced 16 Flash findings, had Sol construct and revise the plan to STABLE, incorporated the Claude Fable 5 external sense-check, split execution into two dependency-safe sprints, and completed the shim sweep (`.oracle/status.md:13-26`). No packification implementation code has changed. Phase 5 execution still awaits the user’s go signal (`.oracle/status.md:1-3,74-77`).
